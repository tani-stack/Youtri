01 / The product

Seven steps. One reasoning pipeline.

Each stage is designed to transform, check, and refine representations before they converge into an output.

01

Field filter

Words become charges. Coulomb flux ranks them and a Gauss gate keeps the strongest signals.

02

Conservation check

An orthogonal rotation and angular impulse restore the system’s moment.

03

Logic induction

Faraday’s law across a sequence turns change into new features.

04

Solve

Candidate cases are weighted and integrated, with complex phasors carrying phase.

05

Chemistry filter

Molecular-orbital bond order and stability rules filter candidate representations.

06

Singularity core

The surviving representations compress into a single vector.

07

Self-evolution

A physics-inspired loss supports bounded self-adaptation during training.
