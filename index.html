04 / FAQ

Questions we hear most.

Is this trace connected to a live model?

No. The interactive examples are prewritten browser-side walkthroughs. A live model trace needs a model inference endpoint; this static site currently has no backend or model API connected.

What are the seven stages?

Field filter, conservation check, logic induction, solve, chemistry filter, singularity core, and self-evolution. These are the project's described physics-inspired stages.

What has been validated so far?

The team reports 92.7% (77 of 83) on its held-out math and physics set and 10 of 10 molecular bond problems. These are project-reported evaluations, not independently verified benchmarks.
