05 / FAQs

Questions we hear most.

What is Youtri’s mission?

To build AI whose reasoning rests on the structure of the physical world, so its answers can be checked rather than trusted.

Who are the founders?

Tanish Parashar leads product, strategy and partnerships. Muaz leads core architecture and ML engineering.

What are the seven stages?

Field filter, conservation check, logic induction, solve, chemistry filter, singularity core and self-evolution. Each stage narrows or verifies the signal before the next one sees it.

How is this different from a Transformer?

X5 is still a causal language model, but its outputs pass through physics-inspired checks, and it learns from worked steps. That pushes it toward computing an answer instead of recalling a pattern.

What has been validated so far?

92.7% (77 of 83) on unseen held-out math and physics problems, 10 of 10 on molecular bond problems, and 72 passing tests. These are our own evaluations, and we plan to publish open benchmarks.

What will the $500k fund?

60% goes to H100 GPU compute for 1B–3B parameter scale, 25% to core ML research and infrastructure, and 15% to open benchmarks.
